The Details

Come join Rick and Jay at Casa Cuale for an end of summer bash. Enjoy some great libations, food, and company.

📅

When: September 25, 2026 · 5:00 – 10:00 PM

📍

Where: Casa Cuale — Cuautémoc 634, Cerro (on the Cuale river, in Gringo Gulch)

🍹

Open bar with Christian and specialty cocktails

👯

OK to bring friends/guests — just please RSVP with the number in your party.

Getting there: It's best to Uber or walk — parking is limited and the street is tight. Walking is about 20-25 min from Los Muertos Beach. Best way: get onto Isla Cuale and head east to the end, take the Iguana bridge (steps) on the left, then right for another 1.5 blocks to the house on the right.
